1. In brief

  • WakeFirst does not ask you to create an account. Alarm plans, selected app tokens, reference-image features, step counts, and morning history are stored and used on your device.
  • Your chosen reference photo is processed on-device with Apple Vision. WakeFirst does not send the photo or its image feature print to a WakeFirst server.
  • Apple processes App Store payments. RevenueCat receives limited purchase and entitlement information so WakeFirst can show plans and verify Premium access; the RevenueCat SDK creates an anonymous app user ID.
  • WakeFirst does not sell personal information, use it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or include an advertising or general-purpose analytics SDK.

2. Who is responsible

“WakeFirst,” “we,” and “us” refer to the developer that publishes the WakeFirst iOS app on the Apple App Store. The seller/developer shown on the WakeFirst App Store product page is the publishing operator and, where privacy law applies, the controller of the processing described in this Policy. Contact our privacy team at matt1232026@gmail.com.

This Policy applies to the WakeFirst iOS app and to these public legal pages. It does not cover Apple, RevenueCat, GitHub, or other third parties when they act as independent controllers for their own services. Their notices explain their own processing.

3. Information and sources

WakeFirst is designed to process most routine information locally. We do not receive information stored only on your device. Information that may be processed by us or our service providers is limited to the following:

  • Purchase and entitlement information: product identifier, store, transaction or receipt-related data, subscription status, purchase and renewal events, and the anonymous RevenueCat App User ID used to associate an entitlement with this installation.
  • Technical request information: app and operating-system version, device/platform type, locale or storefront, and network metadata (including IP address) needed to make a request to Apple, RevenueCat, or the legal-page host and protect those services.
  • Support messages: if you contact us, the email address, message, and any details or attachments you choose to provide so we can respond and resolve the issue.
  • Legal-page visit data: when you open these static legal pages, GitHub Pages may process standard request and security logs, such as IP address, browser/device information, requested page, and time of access.

Apple receives information needed to provide App Store transactions and system services under Apple’s own terms. WakeFirst does not receive your payment-card number.

4. Information that stays on your device

WakeFirst stores the following in its iOS app container or shared app group so the app and its Apple system extensions can perform the routine you configure:

  • plan names, wake times, repeat days, mission settings, durations, and local session/history records;
  • opaque Family Controls tokens for apps you select, used to apply the app-blocking schedule through Apple’s system frameworks;
  • your selected reference photo and a Vision feature-print representation used to compare the object during a Scan Mission;
  • step counts read locally from Core Motion while you complete a Walk Mission; and
  • cached subscription status used to show Premium features.

WakeFirst does not upload your reference photo, image feature print, selected app tokens, step count, alarm plan, or local history to its own server. Apple operating-system services may process information under Apple’s control when you grant the related permission. iOS may include app data in device backups according to your Apple Account and device backup settings; Apple controls those backups.

You can change or delete plans in the app. Deleting WakeFirst removes its local data from that installation, subject to any device backup you have enabled. It does not cancel an App Store subscription or delete Apple’s or RevenueCat’s purchase records.

5. Purchases and RevenueCat

WakeFirst uses Apple’s App Store for billing and RevenueCat’s SDK to load available plans, validate purchases, restore purchases, and determine whether the Premium entitlement is active. Because WakeFirst does not create an account or provide a custom user ID, RevenueCat generates an anonymous App User ID for the app installation.

For these functions, RevenueCat may process the anonymous App User ID, product and entitlement identifiers, transaction or receipt-related data, purchase status and event timestamps, app/platform and OS version, locale/storefront information, and connection metadata such as IP address. RevenueCat’s SDK and its privacy documentation explain the technical data it processes. RevenueCat states that it acts as a processor for end-user information and stores customer data on AWS in the United States; its terms and data-processing terms govern its handling for WakeFirst. Apple processes the payment method, billing, taxes, refunds, and the App Store transaction.

WakeFirst does not send your name, email address, or advertising identifier to RevenueCat from the app code inspected for this Policy. We do not use RevenueCat data for targeted advertising or to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

9. Retention and deletion

  • On-device routine data: remains on the device until you delete or change it, remove the app, or remove the app’s data; Apple backups may persist according to your backup settings.
  • Purchase and entitlement records: retained by Apple and RevenueCat for purchase verification, restoration, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and legal obligations, according to their retention rules. We request deletion from a service provider when applicable law gives you that right and the record is no longer needed for a permitted purpose.
  • Support messages: retained as needed to answer you, maintain a support record, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.
  • Legal-page logs: retained by the host according to its security and retention practices.

We do not have a cloud account containing your alarm plan or morning history. If you request access or deletion, we can only retrieve information held by us or our service providers; we cannot remotely read or delete information stored only on your device.
